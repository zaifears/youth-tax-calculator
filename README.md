# Bangladesh Youth Tax Calculator (`youth-tax-calculator`)
### Specialized for: Students, Interns, Fresh Graduates & Young Employees on `etaxnbr.gov.bd`

An authoritative, battle-tested Antigravity AI pair-programming skill for navigating Bangladesh's **Income Tax Act 2023**, the **Finance Act (AY 2026–2027)**, the official **NBR eReturn System User Manual**, and the mandatory **NBR e-Return portal ([etaxnbr.gov.bd](https://etaxnbr.gov.bd))**.

---

## Why This Skill Exists

Young people entering the tax system in Bangladesh face unique scenarios that generic tax guides and standard accountants completely misunderstand:
- **Academic Stipends / Scholarships:** Claiming 100% tax exemption under the Sixth Schedule, Part 1, Paragraph 8 (e.g. BUP, DU, institutional grants) by unlocking the hidden `Tax Exempted Income` UI tab.
- **Internships vs. Salaried Employment:** Proper classification under Employment with automatic statutory 1/3rd salary exemption.
- **High Bank Turnover Defense:** Explaining BDT 15–30+ Lakhs in bank deposits from shared student competitions (Accolyze, Excelerate), family support, and peer expense splitting with **zero tax liability**.
- **Youth Micro-Investments:** Reconciling Shariah crowdfunding (Biniyog.io, Wegro), SEC-approved mutual funds (EDGE AMC), and multi-broker BO account stock trading losses without triggering audit red flags.
- **Section 70 Loss Carry-Forward:** Correctly handling carried forward capital losses across multiple brokerages (e.g. NBL Securities, IDLC Securities) and explaining the system-generated BDT discrepancy on Page 9 vs. Page 10 of the NBR draft return.
- **Zero-Difference Balance Sheet Math (IT-10B / IT-10BB):** Exact formulas to balance opening wealth, parental gifts under Section 56(g), living expenses, and closing assets down to the exact single Taka.
- **Official NBR User Manual Integration:** Pre-packaged with NBR's official `UserManualEN.pdf` and `Special_Registration.pdf` for overseas taxpayers.

## How to Install on Any PC

### Option 1: One-Click Automatic Install (Recommended)

#### **On Windows (PowerShell):**
Open PowerShell in this directory and run:
```powershell
.\install.ps1
```

#### **On macOS / Linux (Bash):**
Open Terminal in this directory and run:
```bash
chmod +x install.sh
./install.sh
```

---

### Option 2: Manual Installation (Global Machine-Wide)

Copy the `skills/youth-tax-calculator` folder directly into your Antigravity global skills directory:

* **Windows:**
  ```text

  ├── SKILL.md

      └── Special_Registration.pdf
  ```
