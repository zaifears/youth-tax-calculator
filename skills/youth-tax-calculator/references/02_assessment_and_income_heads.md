Income tax assessment is the process of collecting and reviewing the information filed by assesses

4.3.2. Description of Above Screen: 
 
Return Scheme: Currently there are two methods for filling return, 1. Universal Self 2. Normal. 
But online income tax return is directly fileable return through Universal Self Mode. 
 
Assessment Year: The assessment year follows the financial/income year, and it is at this time 
that tax returns are filed. The Assessment year is the year that comes after the Income year. This 
is the time in which the income earned during income year is assessed and taxed. For instance, 
for income year 2021-2022, the assessment year is 2022-2023 
 
Income Year: The year or time period in which money is earned is referred to as the financial or 
income year. An income year is the period between 01 July to 30 June -the accounting year in 
which you earn or income. 
 
Any taxable income in the above-mentioned income year: Among the types of Taxable income 
are the person’s salary or wages, bonuses, interest from investment. Taxable income is the 
amount of a person’s income. So, if you have income then you will give Yes to this option 
 
Any income which is fully exempted tax: Government exempts certain incomes from tax, no 
matter how much you earn from this sectors you don’t have to pay any income tax in these 
sectors, such as freelancing, digital marketing, software business. If you have such income then 
click Yes option, otherwise click No 
 
Resident status: An individual is treated as a Resident of Bangladesh if that person is stayed in 
Bangladesh for 182 days or more in any income year; or 90 days or more in an income for their 
year if that person has previously resided in Bangladesh for a period of more than 365 days during 
the 4 preceding years. If you are resident of Bangladesh, then click on Resident option.

4.4. Source of Income 
Income refers to the money that a person receives in exchange for their labor or investment and 
the sources from which money is earned is called source of income. The income sources are 
combined into Seven basic components. 
This is the income sector or sources, from the seven “Head of Income” select only your sources of 
income. This taxpayer has two types of income sources in the income year. 
 
4.4.1. Description of Above Screen 
1. Salaries: Generally, the Basic pay, Dearness allowance, Conveyance allowance, House rent 
allowance, medical allowance, Overtime allowance, Bonus, Performance bonus, Provident fund, 
Entertainment, and miscellaneous benefits received by an employed taxpayer are taxable income 
of Salary sector, some of which are exempt from tax according to NBR guideline -such as 
conveyance, house rent, medical etc. If you are doing any job like government, private, teaching 
etc., then select this part. 
 
2. Interest on Security: Interest earned on Debenture of Bonds or Securities (e.g., T&T Bond, 
National Investment Bond, Treasury bond/bill) issued by the government and interest on Savings 
Investments (Sanchayapatra) issued by the National Savings Directorate can be shown as income

3. Income from House Property: If a taxpayer rents out his house for residential or commercial

vehicles, computers, furniture, ornaments etc. will not be included as capital assets.

from Deposit Pension Scheme (DPS). A maximum investment of Taka 60,000 per year in DPS with

total wealth of an individual taxpayer is 40 lakh taka or more, it is mandatory to show the assets-
liabilities and income-expenditure account in this form. 
If the total assets amount is less than 40 lakh takas, it is not mandatory to fill it. But if you want to 
make your return clearer you can fill it.

4.8.2. Description of Above Screen: 
 
Basic Salary: Basic Salary is fully taxable income. Whatever the basic salary is, all of it will be added 
to your taxable income. 
 
House Rent Allowance: In case of cash house rent allowance, 50% of basic salary or Tk. 25,000, 
whichever is lower is tax free. Example: If the basic salary of Mr. Shakil Mahmud is 80,000 taka 
per month, 50% is 40,000-taka, 12 X 40,000 = 4,80,000 taka per year. Again 12 X 25,0000 = 3,00000 
Tk., now 4,80,000-3,00000 = 1,80,0000 Tk. This amount will be added to his total taxable income 
 
Medical Allowance: 10% of basic salary or Tk 1,20,000 per year (Tk 10 lakhs in case of disabled 
persons), whichever is less, is exempt from tax. For example: Mr. Shakil Mahmud is getting 
Tk.5,000 per month as medical allowance, but 10% of his monthly basic salary is Tk.8,000. As the 
amount of exemption is higher (8,000 Tk.) here, he is getting full exemption i.e., Tk 5,000, the 
medical allowance will not be added to the taxable income. 
 
Conveyance Allowance: Tax free up to Tk 30,000 per year. For example: Mr. Shakil Mahmud is 
earning 6000 Tk. per month, then he is getting 72000 Tk. per year, since the Income Tax Act 
exempts up to 30,000 Tk., then after deducting 30,000 Tk. from 72,000 Tk., the remaining 42,000 
Tk. will be added to the taxable income. 
 
Festival Bonus: Festival allowance is fully taxable income. Whatever the festival bonus is, all of it 
will be added to your taxable income.

4.11. Non-Cash Benefit 
A Non-Cash Benefit is any benefit that an employer pays for an employee that is of a private nature, 
for example as part of an employee’s contract they may be provided with a rent-free house and a 
vehicle for private use. 
 
 
Note: The taxpayer doesn’t get any Non-Cash Benefits from his company. 
 
4.11.1. Description of above screen: 
Rent free Accommodation: Rent free accommodation means a place of residence which the 
employer gives to an employee and the employee’s family to stay in. 
 
Accommodation at Concessional Rate: In case an employer provides accommodation to an 
employee at a concessional rate. Rent free accommodation can be given either free or at a 
concessional rate. 
 
Vehicle Facility Provided: In some organizations, employers provide vehicles to their employees 
for transportation. This will be counted as a perquisite offered to the employee by the 
organization. 
 
Other Non-Cash Benefit: When it comes to medical related benefit, the top non-cash benefit is 
Health Insurance.
