# NBR e-Return: Income Heads & Statutory Exemptions (Chapter 4)
*Source: NBR eReturn System User Manual (Pages 27-41) & Income Tax Act 2023*

## 1. Employment (Schedule 1 / Section 32)
- Statutory Deduction: Lower of 1/3rd gross employment receipts or BDT 4,50,000 is automatically exempt.
- Enter under Private/Other than Govt Pay Scale.

## 2. Financial Assets (Section 35)
- Bank Interest/Profit: Declare gross interest. Claim bank TDS credit.
- Dividends: Declare gross dividend. Claim 10%/15% TDS credit.
- P2P Crowdfunding / Sukuk: Disclose under Interest From Any Other Securities/Financial Assets.

## 3. Capital Gains (Sections 57, 70, 72)
- Select Transfer of share of listed Company (Individual).
- **UI Quirk:** Must click the **Green Checkmark (✓)** to render the input fields.
- Enter BO ID, broker name, and gross gain/loss. Accepts negative numbers (e.g. -3924).
- Section 70 Carry-Forward: Unabsorbed capital losses carry forward for 6 years. Cannot offset salary/interest.

## 4. Other Sources (Section 38)
- Honorarium, academic prize awards, consulting. Declare individual proportionate share only.

## 5. Tax-Exempted Income (Sixth Schedule, Part 1, Para 8)
- Student stipends/scholarships to meet education costs are 100% tax-exempt.
- Select Other Exemption under 6th Schedule Part 1 -> Para Any Other Exemption.
