4.12. Rebate 
 
If the taxpayer has investments in certain sectors, the taxpayer gets investment tax rebate. The tax 
payable figure is obtained by subtracting the tax rebate figure from the Tax leviable on total Taxable 
income. If the taxable income of a taxpayer is Tk 5,16,140, then the tax on this income will be Tk 
16,614, after deducting the rebate from this figure (15,664-Rebate), the amount that will come will 
be his payable tax for that year. 
4.13. Investment Category 
There are three categories of investments of the taxpayer: 
 
1. Life Insurance Premium 
2. Deposit Pension Scheme (DPS) 
3. Approved Savings Certificate (Sanchayapatra)

4.14. Expenditure (IT-10BB) 
Every individual taxpayer is required to file a statement of living expenses along with his 
return of income. However, in the case of individual taxpayers who have income from salary 
or business or professional sector, if the total income of the relevant income year does not 
exceed three lakh Taka, the submission of the statement of expenditure shall not be 
mandatory.

4.16. Expense Relating to Lifestyle 
1. Expense For food, Clothing, and other Essentials: This sequence has to provide information 
on maintenance expenses of the taxpayer and his dependent family members. 
 
2. Accommodation Expense: This sequence should provide information on Housing expenses. 
*If you do not live in a rented house, you should give the information about your own house, 
Parents' house, Employer's house or someone else's house in the ‘Comment box’. *Home 
maintenance expenses such as municipal tax, service charges etc. if any, should be entered

here. *If you Reside at your own House or Flat, accommodation Expenses will be blank and, 
in the comment, you have to write ‘Reside at own Flat’. 
 
3. Auto and Transportation Expense: All expenses related to the vehicle, such as fuel, 
maintenance, driver's salary, etc. information should be provided here. 
 
4. Household and Utility Expense: Electricity bill, Gas bill, water bill, sewage and daily waste 
disposal charges, residential telephone bill, household helper, all the information should be 
given here. 
 
5. Children’s Education Expenses: Children's education expenses information should be given. 
 
6. Special Expenses: Festivals, events, gifts, domestic and foreign travel, vacations, donations, 
humanitarian aid and other special expenses should be given here. 
 
7. Any other Expenses: If there is any other expenditure other than the expenses mentioned 
in 1 to 6 mentioned above, the expenses or medical expenses should be entered in this box. 
 
8. Total Expense Relating to Lifestyle: Sum of expenses from serial number 1 to 7.
