# NBR e-Return: Schedule 5 Investment Tax Rebate
*Source: NBR eReturn System User Manual (Pages 42-43, 65-66) & ITA 2023 Sixth Schedule Part 2*

## Rebate Calculation Formula
\text{Rebate} = \min(15\% \times \text{Eligible Investment}, 3\% \times \text{Taxable Income}, \text{BDT } 10,00,000)

## Eligible Investments:
1. SEC-approved Open-End / Closed-End Mutual Funds (at purchase cost).
2. DPS up to BDT 1,20,000/year.
3. Secondary market listed stocks.
4. Life insurance premium (up to 10% of sum assured).
5. Approved Zakat Fund.
*Note: Rebate cannot create negative tax liability or cash refund.*
