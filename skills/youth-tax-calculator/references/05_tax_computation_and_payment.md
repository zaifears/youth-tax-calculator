6.3. Tax Computation 
Gross Tax: Gross means exclusive of deductions, tax leviable on Total income. If someone's total 
taxable income is Tk. 5,16,140, then his gross tax will be Tk 16,614. 
Gross Tax Calculation: 
Zero (0) tax on first Tk. 3,00,000, tax at 5% on next Tk. 1,00,000 = Tk. 5,000, 
Tax at the rate of 10% on the remaining Tk. 1,16,140 = Tk. 11,614, 
Therefore, total tax will be Tk. (0 + 5,000 + 11,614) = Tk. 16,614 
 
Tax Rebate: Taxpayer gets ‘Investment Tax Rebate’ if the taxpayer has investments/contributions 
in certain sector (For example, the maximum annual investment in DPS is Tk. 60,000, Life insurance 
Premium, Sanchayapatra). The figure of tax payable is obtained by deducting the figure of tax 
Rebate from the figure of income tax chargeable on the total income.

6.4. Total Amount Payable screen 
 
6.4.1. Description of Above Screen: 
Minimum Tax: Minimum tax is determined depending on the location of the taxpayer. The minimum 
income tax payable in respect of income above the tax-free limit is as follows region-wise: 
1. Dhaka North, South and Chattogram city corporation area 5,000 Taka 
2. Other City corporation area 4,000 Taka 
3. Areas other than City Corporation 3,000 Taka 
A taxpayer who has income above the tax-free limit- a) If the amount of income tax payable is less 
than the applicable minimum income tax amount, or b) The amount of income tax payable excluding 
tax Rebate is less than the applicable minimum income tax, even if it is zero or negative, the taxpayer 
has to pay the minimum income tax applicable to him. 
Surcharge: In case of a normal individual taxpayer, the surcharge will be payable on the basis of 
assets shown in the statement of Assets, Liabilities and Expenses. Surcharge rate is zero up to 3 crore 
Taka.

Advance Income Tax (AIT): 
If the taxpayer has paid Advance Tax, then the amount of tax paid should be shown in the income 
tax return. Suppose a normal individual taxpayer has paid Tk. 25,000 as advance tax during fitness 
renewal of his car, he has to show this in his tax return, otherwise he cannot claim credit of advance 
tax paid. 
Payment with Return: 
The amount of tax payable by a taxpayer in a year, if he has paid any tax before filing the 
return, then that amount should be deducted from the total tax and the remaining amount 
should be deposited with the return by automated challan (A Challan) or e-Payment system 
if the amount up to 5 laks taka.

automated verification process):
