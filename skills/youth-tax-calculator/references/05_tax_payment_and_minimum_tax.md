# NBR e-Return: Tax Computation & Payment (Chapter 6)
*Source: NBR eReturn System User Manual (Pages 63-80)*

## Progressive Tax Slabs (General Individual)
- First BDT 3,50,000 - 4,00,000: 0%
- Slabs progressively graduated.

## Minimum Tax Floor Condition (Manual Page 67)
- Minimum tax is BDT 5,000 (Dhaka/Ctg), BDT 4,000 (Other City Corp), BDT 3,000 (Non-City Corp).
- **CRITICAL CONDITION:** Minimum tax ONLY applies if Net Taxable Income EXCEEDS the statutory tax-free ceiling. If taxable income <= ceiling, tax is strictly BDT 0.00!

## Source Tax Refundable
- When Tax Payable is 0.00 and TDS was deducted by banks/companies, the portal registers the entire TDS under Source Tax Refundable.
